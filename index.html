We started with a simple idea: a paint shop where the staff actually knows paint. Not just brand names and prices — but which finish works on a damp wall, how many coats a dark shade really needs, and why primer isn't optional. That knowledge built our reputation, one living room at a time.

Why Birla Opus

We chose to partner with Birla Opus because their product range covers every real-world scenario our customers face — from luxury interior finishes to brutal exterior weatherproofing. We've tested their lines extensively and stand behind every tin we sell.
